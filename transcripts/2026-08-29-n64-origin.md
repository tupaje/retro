## Session

Gaming started in 2014.

2016: bought about 40 games. A few years after that, the cart pile was 60 to 80.

The first five beaten to credits were Super Mario 64, Pilotwings 64, Shadows of the Empire, and Star Fox 64. After those, the aim became the whole set — 296 games, his count.

2017: first EverDrive, full library on the cart. 2019: sold the physical games and bought the second EverDrive. Early 2021: the Nintendo 64 Anthology book. Also watched a long run of N64 reviews and retrospectives that tracked how the games were made. That was prep, not the run.

Around 2021, third EverDrive, ordered while the war in Ukraine was on. The worry was whether the package would make it out.

Official run started October 2022. Game one was Pilotwings 64. Midway through that first game the rule became beat-to-credit. After the first sports game, the rule changed again: not every title is worth beat-to-credit.

The full N64 pass took eight months. English Aleck 64, English 64DD, and prototype games were in. Homebrew was not.

Then two years off games.

Spring 2025: decision to continue the other consoles through emulators. Next smallest library is Virtual Boy.

## Why it matters to the journey

The N64 pass is where the project stopped being a pile of carts and became a set of rules — written, then rewritten twice in the first stretch of the official run. Selling the games and living on flash carts is part of that, not a side note.
