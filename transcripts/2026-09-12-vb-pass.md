## Played

Virtual Boy was the first of the 2025 consoles, about two years after the N64 pass ended in 2023. Emulated. Not the headset. Not a flash cart in the native system.

Fourteen official titles. Two days. Nothing skipped inside the rule.

## Rule

Going forward: play every *unique official software experience* from the console's lifetime.

In:
- licensed games released while the console was still sold

Out:
- re-releases
- aftermarket
- homebrew
- prototypes
- unlicensed

On this pass the work was: play each title, learn the mechanics, gauge the experience. The rule did not change mid-console.
